### About

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow in Computational Neurosciences at the Department of [Brain and Behavioral Sciences](https://dbbs.dip.unipv.it/) (DBBS) at the University of Pavia, Italy. My research is built on the same core workflow: atlas-driven reconstruction and simulation of brain circuits, from the whole mouse brain to cerebellar microcircuits in healthy and pathological states. 

I have a PhD in computational neuroscience from Blue Brain Project - EPFL, Switzerland (2022), a Master degree in Artificial intelligence from Université Claude Bernard Lyon, France (2016) and a Master degree in Computer Sciences Engineering from Polytech Lyon, France.

My latest postdoctoral position involved adapting neuron morphologies used in brain circuit modeling to atlas-based volumes
